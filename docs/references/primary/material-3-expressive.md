# Material Design 3 Expressive reference pack

## Source and licensing

- Official design system: https://m3.material.io/
- Official Material Web source: https://github.com/material-components/material-web

Design guidance and code/assets have different rights. This repository stores analysis and links only; it does not copy Material assets. Verify the upstream artifact license before reuse.

## Transferable strengths

- Semantic tonal color systems rather than one hard-coded accent.
- Motion, shape, and color working together to communicate hierarchy and emotion.
- Strong state feedback and adaptive behavior.
- Expressive moments can improve desirability without reducing clarity.

## Local adoption

- Violet, blue, graphite, and green are full semantic themes, not monochrome recolors.
- Expressive mode allows stronger entrance, completion, progress, and onboarding moments.
- Gradients and shape contrast are concentrated in hero, progress, AI, and marketing moments.
- Productive work areas keep calmer controls and surfaces.

## Rejected defaults

- Android-specific patterns are not web requirements.
- Large floating actions and extreme roundness are not general defaults.
- Expressive motion is not applied continuously to dense tables or repeated controls.
- Decorative shape changes cannot hide state or reduce target clarity.

## Influenced artifacts

- `src/styles/tokens.css` theme gradients and motion modes
- `src/styles/index.css` hero and interactive feedback
- Marketing, onboarding, progress, and AI Golden Examples
